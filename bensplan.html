Ben's Game Plan

Weekly Training Blueprint

Putting to driver — every part of your game, every day this week.

This Week
✓6 training days (Mon–Sat)
✓1 rest day (Sun)
✓Putting drills every day
✓Distance wedge work every day
✓Pre-shot routine on every single shot
The Week, Day by Day
MON

Monday

Putting baseline + short game foundation

4-Foot Putting Baseline
Make as many 4-foot putts in a row as you can (chalk line). Use your pre-shot routine on every putt. Record the number.
Target: 20+ in a row
Chipping Ladder
Chips from three distances: 10 yards (5 chips), 15 yards (5 chips), 20 yards (5 chips). Pre-shot routine every chip.
Target: 9+ of 15 within 3 ft
Distance Wedges
40, 60, 80 yards — 10 balls each. Feel where the club needs to go in your backswing before you swing. No scraping and hitting.
Target: 27+ of 30 solid
9-Hole Play
Play 9 holes. Pre-shot routine on every single shot. No score pressure — just focus on execution.
Target: 4+ greens hit
TUE

Tuesday

Long putt control + distance wedge refinement

Lag Putting
Putts from 18, 24, 32, 40 feet. Pre-shot routine on every putt. Every putt should finish within 3 feet of the hole.
Target: 28+ of 40 in safe zone
Distance Wedges
40, 60, 80 yards — 10 balls each. Feel your backswing before you swing. Pre-shot routine every shot.
Target: 27+ of 30
Driver Work
30 drivers. Pre-shot routine on every driver. Centered contact — TrackMan smash factor 1.46+ if available.
Target: 28+ centered
9-Hole Play
Play 9 holes. Keep score. Pre-shot routine every shot.
Target: 4+ greens hit
WED

Wednesday

Full 18-hole practice

18-Hole Round
Play 18 holes. Keep score. Pre-shot routine on every single shot — treat this as a practice tournament.
Target: 9+ greens hit (50%)
THU

Thursday

Pressure & mental toughness

Short Putting (Pressure)
20 putts from 3–6 feet. Pre-shot routine on every putt. Treat the last 10 as tournament pressure.
Target: 16+ makes (80%+)
Lag Putting
30 lag putts (18–40 feet). Pre-shot routine every putt. All balls should finish in the 3-foot safe zone.
Target: 24+ in safe zone
Distance Wedges (Pressure)
40, 60, 80 yards — 10 balls each. Feel your backswing. Simulate tournament pressure on every shot.
Target: 27+ of 30
9-Hole Play
Play 9 holes. Keep score. Focus: the final 3 holes matter most.
Target: last 3 ≥ first 6
FRI

Friday

Distance wedge mastery + putting work

Distance Wedges (All Ranges)
40, 60, 80, 100 yards — 10 balls each. Feel your backswing and commit to it. Pre-shot routine every shot.
Target: 38+ of 40
Short Putting
20 putts from 3–6 feet. Pre-shot routine every putt. Make as many as possible.
Target: 16+ makes
Lag Putting
30 lag putts (18–40 feet). Pre-shot routine on every putt. All balls in the 3-foot safe zone.
Target: 24+ in safe zone
9-Hole Play
Play 9 holes. Keep score. Pre-shot routine every shot — apply everything from this week.
Target: 5+ greens hit
SAT

Saturday

Reset & measure improvement

4-Foot Putting (Week Repeat)
Same drill as Monday. Record the number, pre-shot routine on every putt, and compare to Monday.
Target: beat Monday's number
Chipping Ladder (Week Repeat)
Same drill as Monday (10/15/20 yard chips). Pre-shot routine every chip. Record accuracy.
Target: beat Monday's accuracy
Driver Work
30 drivers. Pre-shot routine on every driver. Centered contact — 1.46+ smash factor if available.
Target: 28+ centered
9-Hole Play
Play 9 holes. Keep score. Pre-shot routine every shot — apply what you learned this week.
Target: 5+ greens hit
SUN

Sunday

Rest & recovery

No Golf Practice
Take the day off from golf. Conditioning or mobility work is fine if you want it.
Week Review
Look back on the week — did you improve? What needs work? What went well?
Plan Next Week
What tournaments are coming up? When will you train next week?

✓GIR 50%+ by end of week.

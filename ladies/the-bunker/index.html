The Bunker Clinic · Ladies’ Session

Make the sand do the work.

Welcome back. Everything we worked on today, in one place — so the bunker stops being the shot you dread and becomes one you know how to play.

You don’t hit the ball out of a bunker. You hit the sand — and the sand throws the ball out.

Today’s Throughline
Part One

Set up to escape.

Half the shot is won before you swing. Build a base you can’t be knocked off, and aim at the right thing.

01 · Setup

Dig in, and open the face.

Work your feet into the sand for a stable base, stand a touch wider than normal, and lay the clubface slightly open so it slides through the sand instead of digging in. Stable base, open face.

02 · Base

Quiet legs — no sway.

Your lower body stays calm and stable the whole way — no sliding side to side. Turn around a steady centre; the moment you sway, the bottom of your swing moves with you and you catch it fat or thin. Turn around a steady centre.

03 · Target

Aim at the sand, not the ball.

Pick a spot a couple of inches behind the ball — that’s what your club actually hits, and the ball rides out on the sand. Keep your eyes on that spot the whole swing. Hit the sand behind it.

Part Two

The swing, top to finish.

Three moves, in order — swing up steep, splash through the bottom, and finish facing the target.

01 · Up
Set it up steep
Set (hinge) your wrists early so the club swings up steep and upright, not low and around your body. That steeper shape lets the club drop down into the sand behind the ball. Set the wrists, swing it up.
02 · Bottom
Splash under it
Slide the club through the sand under the ball, keeping the face open and looking up at the sky. Take a thin, shallow slice and keep accelerating — the bounce does the lifting. Slide it under, face to the sky.
03 · Finish
Belt to target
Rotate all the way to a tall, balanced finish with your belt buckle at the target. Quitting at the ball is the number-one reason it stays in — so finish every one. Belt buckle to the target.

Part Three

Take it to the practice bunker.

Two drills to groove the splash, and one game to melt the fear for good.

Drill 01

Draw the Line

No ball. Draw a line in the sand, then make swings that enter behind the line and exit in front of it, splashing sand to a full finish. Pure low-point training, no pressure.

Drill 02

Dollar Bill

Imagine a dollar bill lying under the ball and splash out the entire bill — not a coin, not a crater. This grooves the shallow, even slice of sand that floats the ball up softly.

The Game

Bunker Confidence Drill

1+ Player Sand Only Practice Bunker

Drop 10 balls in the practice bunker. Your only goal: get 5 of them out and onto the green — just out and up, no target yet. Count how many balls it takes, then play again and beat your number. Confidence comes from reps, not perfection.

A coach who listens first.

I've spent nearly twenty years on the lesson tee, coaching golfers across Canada. The best thing I've learned is that no two golfers are the same.

So I start by listening. I want to understand how you move, how you think, and what you want from the game before I suggest a single change. My coaching is guided, not prescriptive. Every golfer deserves my full attention and best effort.

Your body shapes your swing.

As a TPI Certified coach, I screen how your body moves before I prescribe any technique. Some positions simply aren't available to some bodies, and no amount of practice will change that. Finding the swing that works for you is faster, and kinder, than forcing one that doesn't.

That matters for everyone: a junior building athletic foundations, an adult who wants to play without pain, or a competitive player chasing the last few shots.

Clarity you can take home.

TrackMan shows us exactly what the ball is doing and why. I'll translate those numbers into plain language and one or two clear things to work on. You'll leave every lesson knowing what we did, why it matters, and how to practise it.

From the range to the course.

The range is comfortable. The course is where it counts. Through Vision54 and years of on-course coaching, I help golfers bring their best swing, and a calmer mind, to the first tee.

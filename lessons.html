Questions.

What happens in the Performance Assessment?

We spend 90 minutes measuring your swing and ball flight with TrackMan, screening how your body moves with TPI, and filming your swing. You leave with a written plan that sets three priorities. Every lesson after that works from that plan.

Which package should I choose?

If you're new to coaching or want a tune-up, start with Foundation. If you're making a real change, like a new swing pattern or a lower handicap, Progression gives it time to stick. Mastery is a full season of coaching. Not sure? Book the assessment through Foundation and we'll talk about it then.

Where are lessons?

From spring to fall, outdoors at Mad River Golf Club in Creemore. In winter, lessons move indoors with TrackMan. The booking page always shows the current location.

Do I need to be a club member?

No. Everyone is welcome, whatever your level.

How soon can I start?

Your assessment is usually scheduled within a week of booking.

I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Some of my favourite students started from scratch. You'll never be rushed or made to feel out of place.

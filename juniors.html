How I coach young golfers.

A ten-year-old is not a small adult. Juniors deserve coaching built for where they are right now.

Right for their age

Coaching follows the stages of long-term athlete development, so a seven-year-old plays and explores while a sixteen-year-old trains to compete.

Body first

As a TPI Junior certified coach, I screen how a young golfer moves before teaching any technique. Growing bodies change fast, and the coaching changes with them.

Parents in the loop

You get a written development profile, regular progress updates, and a player portal where your junior tracks rounds and practice.

Questions from parents.

What age can my child start?

As young as five. The coaching is matched to your child's age and stage, from playful movement skills for the youngest golfers to tournament preparation for teenagers.

Is Ryan qualified to coach juniors?

Yes. Ryan is a CPGA Class A Professional, an NCCP Certified Coach and TPI Junior certified. Through the PGA of Canada he has completed Safe Sport Training and Respect in Sport, and holds a current police record check.

How do junior lesson packages work?

Choose any of the three packages and enter the code JUNIOR100 at checkout for $100 off. Every package starts with a Performance Assessment, and you'll get a written development profile afterwards.

Can parents watch?

Yes. Parents join the first and last sessions of every package, and I'll always make time to talk through progress and what to practise at home.

Where are lessons?

At Mad River Golf Club in Creemore: outdoors from spring to fall, and indoors with TrackMan through the winter.

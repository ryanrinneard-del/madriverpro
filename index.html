How coaching works.

  1. Assess

    We measure your swing, your ball flight, and how your body moves. No guessing.

  2. Plan

    You get a written plan with three clear priorities, in plain language.

  3. Build

    Each lesson works on one priority, with practice you can do on your own.

  4. Play

    We take it to the course, so it holds up when the score counts.

A coach who listens first.

I've spent nearly twenty years on the lesson tee, and the best thing I've learned is that no two golfers are the same. I want to understand how you move, how you think, and what you want from the game before I suggest a single change.

You'll leave every lesson knowing what we worked on, why it matters, and exactly how to practise it. Whether it's your first lesson or your hundredth, you'll be welcomed and never rushed.
